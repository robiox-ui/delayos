# FlowOS launch video (4Tyj6ZfUORk): frame-by-frame analysis

Video: "Microsoft Said Your PC Is Too Old, So We Built Our Own OS" (4:19). Source: 29 contact sheets (sheet_000 to sheet_028) at 1 fps, every sheet viewed. Key frames were also cropped and upscaled 3x to read small UI text.

Legibility: quoted text is exact where it was readable. "~" marks a best guess from a low-resolution thumbnail.

Structure of the video:
- **0:00 to 0:50:** an animated mock-up of the desktop with a notch, glass dock, Start menu, Control Center and Dynamic Island.
- **0:51 to 2:51:** recordings labelled "Real FlowOS screen", with a small pill top-left (red dot plus "Real FlowOS screen"). Each recording sits in a rounded frame on a blurred copy of the wallpaper. Every caption has two lines: a small pink category label (for example "Liquid glass", "Control Center", "Settings") and a white bold sentence under it.
- **2:52 to 3:24:** footage labelled "Real footage · VM with 128 MB of RAM". A speed badge sits top-right ("8× speed", "Real time", "3× speed", "5× speed", "4× speed").
- **3:25 to end:** the hardware-requirements slide, the installer, a highlights reel, and the end card.

## 2. Visual design spec (to reproduce, unbranded)

### Global look
- The whole UI is translucent "liquid glass": heavy background blur (roughly 30 to 60 px Gaussian), a saturation boost, a thin light 1 px inner border/rim highlight, and a soft drop shadow.
  - Dark variant: a charcoal/navy tint at about 55 to 70% opacity.
  - Light variant: a white/peach frosted tint.
- Marketing cards also show a diagonal specular glare stripe.
- **Accent:** raspberry pink. Primary ≈ #F0436E/#F2476F, dark partner ≈ #A3173F/#9E1B3F. It is used for selected states, toggles, icon backgrounds, badges, and the active desktop number.
  - The installer uses a warmer orange-red (≈ #D8452B) and a blue (≈ #2F6FD6) for the Windows partition. The VM lite session uses an orange dot and salmon heat-tints.
- **Typography:** a geometric grotesk similar to Inter Display / SF Pro / "Inter Tight". Headlines are heavy (700 to 800) with tight tracking, and UI text is regular/medium. Captions use the same family.
- **Corner radii:**
  - Dock pills and island: fully rounded capsules.
  - Panels (Start, Control Center): about 16 to 20 px.
  - Tiles inside the Control Center: about 12 to 14 px.
  - Windows: about 10 to 12 px.
  - Icon squircles: about 22%.
- **Icon style:** glossy rounded-square app icons in pink/crimson and charcoal (Store pink bag with "V", Settings pink gear on black, Task Manager pink line chart on black, Terminal ">_" on black, Calculator pink dots on black, Android green phone on black, VLC pink play button on black, Steam grey gamepad). Files is a pink folder and Notepad is a white notepad with a pink header and pencil. Monochrome line icons are used for system glyphs. In the real build, settings-nav icons are white glyphs on pink rounded squares.

### Top bar (macOS-like)
- Full width and thin (about 24 to 28 px at 1080p), made of glass.
- **Left:** the logo plus the **active window/app name** ("Desktop", "Settings", "Task Manager", "VLC media player", "Telegram", "Obsidian", "GNU Image Manipulation Program").
- **Right:** a display/network icon, a speaker icon, and the date/time in the format **"Mon 2 Nov 09:41"** (abbreviated weekday, day, abbreviated month, 24 h time).
- **Centre:** a black pill "notch", the Dynamic Island.

### Dynamic Island
- At rest it is a small black capsule (about 64×12 px) at the top centre, overlapping the top bar.
- On a notification:
  - **Mock-up:** it expands into a wide black capsule holding the app icon, a bold title, a grey subtitle and an inline pink action button ("Open").
  - **Real build:** a translucent glass capsule drops from the top showing app icon, "App · now", a bold title and a secondary line.
- It collapses back after a few seconds. Example content: "Spotify is ready / Installed in 42 seconds".

### Dock / taskbar
- A floating, centred glass capsule at the bottom with a margin of about 8 to 10 px. There is **no full-width bar**, and windows do not cover it.
- **Real order:** Start (logo), Search, Task View, then pinned/running apps (Files, Notepad, Calculator, Task Manager, Settings, and running apps appended). Running indicators are small dots or underlines.
- A small separate round glass button sits in the bottom-right corner (notifications or show desktop).
- **Mock-up variant:** the dock is split into three glass groups: apps, quick status (Wi-Fi, volume, battery), and a clock tile ("09:41 / 02/11/2026").
- **VM lite variant:** the same three-group layout in flat light style, with a clock tile "08:55 / 11/2/26" and a bell icon.

### Start menu (Windows 11 layout in glass)
- Centred above the dock, about 600×520 px.
- Search field "Search apps, files and the web" (pink focus ring in the real build).
- "Pinned" header with an "All Apps >" chip, and a 6-column icon grid.
- "Recommended" section (empty state: "Files you open will show up here.").
- Footer with the avatar initial and user name at left and a power button at right.

### Control Center (Super+A)
- A glass panel anchored top-right under the clock.
- A 2×2 toggle area: one tall card with Network and Bluetooth stacked, and separate cards for Dark Mode and Focus. Each item is a circular icon (pink when on, grey when off) with a title and a status line.
- Sliders for Display (brightness) and Sound (with a "100%" readout); the sliders are thick white pills.
- A row of four square buttons: Snip, Settings, Lock, Power.
- A "Desktops" row with virtual desktop buttons 1 to 4.

### Windows
- Title text sits left (small app icon plus title). Controls are **Windows-style** (minimise, maximise, close) at the top-right inside a small rounded group.
- Dark app surfaces ≈ #17161C/#1C1B22, with cards slightly lighter (#22212A) and rounded about 8 px. The selected nav item gets a subtle fill and a pink left accent bar.
- Android windows add a back arrow at top-left and a rotate button.

### Light/dark
- Both modes are shown. Dark is the default; light mode turns the panels white-frosted with dark text.
- The VM lite session and the installer use a light theme with a peach/terracotta palette.

### Wallpapers used (nature photography set)
1. Snowy peak mirrored in a lake (Patagonia, blue).
2. Emerald alpine lake with forest (Jasna, Slovenia).
3. Aurora over a Lofoten village at night.
4. Golden desert dunes.
5. Coastal cliffs at sunset (Big Sur), used for light mode and the end card.
6. Dolomites at dusk over a forest.
7. Sunbeams in a redwood forest.
8. Green sea cliffs at sunset.
9. Autumn mountains with a lake.
10. Peach/salmon abstract silk waves (installer).
11. Flat peach-to-terracotta gradient (lite session).

### Motion
- Text blur-in per word.
- Circular "portal" reveal wipes between wallpapers.
- Island expand/collapse.
- Panels zoom in from their anchor.
- Keycap highlight animation.

## 3. Feature list and likely Linux/KDE implementation

| Feature (as shown) | Likely component / how to reproduce |
|---|---|
| Glass top bar + floating dock + blur ("Liquid glass") | KDE Plasma 6 (Wayland) with KWin blur (or the "Better Blur" / kwin-effects-forceblur effect, with high strength and saturation), plus custom Plasma panels: a top panel and a floating, fit-content bottom panel. Alternatively a custom QML/Quickshell shell. Task Manager lists a **"FlowOS desktop"** process (115 MB), which suggests a custom shell process. KDE pieces still running are baloo_file, gmenudbusmenuproxy, at-spi and dconf. |
| Active app name in top bar | Plasma "Window Title" applet (or a Global Menu without the menu). |
| Dynamic Island (notch + notifications) | A custom QML layer-shell surface (Quickshell, or a Plasma widget/panel). It hooks org.freedesktop.Notifications, replacing Plasma's notification popups. |
| Clock "Mon 2 Nov 09:41" | Plasma Digital Clock with custom date format `ddd d MMM`, 24 h. |
| Windows 11-style centred Start menu with Pinned/Recommended/All Apps/search | Custom Kickoff fork or a "Menu 11"-style plasmoid restyled. Search via KRunner/Milou; Recommended via KActivities recent documents. |
| Task view button | KWin Overview effect. |
